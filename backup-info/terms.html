Backup terms of use

Purdue Orbital Backups is for authorized club administration.

Authorized use

Only connect accounts and back up information you are authorized to administer. Protect the Google account, GitHub repository, automation credentials, and recovery key. Do not share backup access with unauthorized people.

Operational limits

Backups depend on Supabase, GitHub Actions, Google Drive, valid credentials, and available storage. Runs can fail or be delayed. No uninterrupted service, fixed recovery time, or complete recovery is promised. Administrators must monitor results, retain the recovery key, and periodically test recovery in an isolated environment.

Recovery and retention

A verified upload confirms matching archive bytes; it does not prove that the application can be restored. Some service settings and secrets require separate records. Administrators manage backup retention and must review recovery steps before modifying any live system.
